Frequently Asked Questions

What is HackHub?

HackHub is a beginner-friendly platform that helps students discover hackathons, form teams, manage tasks, and showcase projects in one place.

Do I need a team to join a hackathon?

No. You can join events as a solo participant. You can also use the event page to find other participants and form a team together.

How do I create or join a team?

After joining an event, go to the Teams tab. You can create a new team and get a team code, or enter an existing team code to join.

What can I do in the team workspace?

Each team has a shared task board and project page, where members can manage tasks, add project details, and collaborate.

How do I submit my project?

Use the Project tab inside your team workspace to add your project title, description, tech stack, demo link, GitHub repo, and screenshots.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click Forgot Password on the login page. You will receive an OTP by email to reset your password.

Is HackHub free?

Yes. HackHub is free for both participants and organizers.

How can I host my own hackathon?

Click Host an Event or Create Event. Fill in the event details such as title, description, date, deadline, and status.
