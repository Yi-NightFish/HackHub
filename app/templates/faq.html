Frequently Asked Questions

What is HackHub?

HackHub is a beginner-friendly platform that helps students discover hackathons, form teams, manage tasks, and showcase projects - all in one place.

Do I need a team to join a hackathon?

No! You can join events as a solo participant. Use the “Solo” tab on any event page to find other solo participants and form a team together.

How do I create or join a team?

After joining an event, go to the “Teams” tab. You can create a new team (you'll get a unique team code) or enter an existing team code to join.

What can I do in the team workspace?

Each team has a shared task board, and a project page (where you can add screenshots, GitHub links, tech stack).

How do I submit my project?

Use the “Project” tab inside your team workspace to add your project title, description, tech stack, demo link, GitHub repo, and screenshots. Organisers will see it at the end of the event.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click “Forgot password?” on the login page. You'll receive an OTP via email to reset your password.

Is HackHub free?

Yes! HackHub is completely free for both participants and organisers.

How can I host my own hackathon?

Click “Host an event” on the homepage or navigate to “Create Event” from your dashboard. Fill in the details (title, description, dates, deadline) and you're ready to go.
