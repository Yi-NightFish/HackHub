About HackHub

Your launchpad into the world of hackathons.

Our Mission

Many beginners, especially students, find hackathons exciting but intimidating. Finding teammates, managing tasks, and understanding the process can feel overwhelming.

HackHub helps beginners discover events, form teams, collaborate on projects, and showcase their final work in one place.

What We Offer

Discover Events

Find beginner-friendly hackathons easily.

Team Formation

Create or join teams using invite codes.

Task Management

Organize work using a Kanban board.

Team Chat

Communicate with teammates in real time.

Project Showcase

Upload your final project and links.

Organizer Tools

Manage hackathons and participants easily.

Who We Serve

Students

For beginners who want to join hackathons, learn new skills, and build real projects.

Organizers

For clubs and organizers who want to manage events, teams, and submissions efficiently.

Teams

For groups who need a shared workspace to plan, assign tasks, and collaborate.

How It Works
1. Create your account
Register and complete your profile.
2. Explore events
Find hackathons that match your interests.
3. Join or create a team
Use a team code or become a team leader.
4. Collaborate
Use tasks and chat to work together.
5. Submit your project
Showcase your final work.
