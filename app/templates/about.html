About HackHub

Your launchpad into the world of hackathons

Our Mission

Many beginners, especially students like us, lack a clear entry point into hackathons, which can make the experience feel intimidating and confusing. Despite this, hackathons are highly valuable as they allow beginners to learn practical skills, collaborate with others, and apply knowledge to real-world problems in a hands-on environment. They also help build strong portfolios and improve resumes.

HackHub aims to bridge this gap by providing a structured and beginner-friendly platform that guides users through joining events, forming teams, and developing projects, making hackathons more accessible and less overwhelming for newcomers.

What We Offer

Discover Events

Browse and join hackathons tailored to beginners. Filter by status, date, and topic.

Team Formation

Create teams, invite members with a unique code, or find solo participants to team up with.

Task Management

Collaborate with your team using shared checklists, assign tasks, set priorities, and track deadlines.

Real-time Chat

Communicate with teammates and other participants through our built-in messaging system.

Project Gallery

Showcase your final projects with screenshots, links, and tech stacks. Get likes and comments from the community.

Organizer Tools

Host your own hackathon, post announcements, monitor participants, and export data for evaluation.

Who We Serve

Students & Beginners

If you're new to hackathons, HackHub gives you everything you need: easy event discovery, team matching, task tracking, and a supportive community.

Organizers & Clubs

Running a hackathon? Use our platform to manage registrations, communicate with participants, track team progress, and generate reports.

How It Works

  1. Create an account - sign up with email, verify OTP, and complete your profile.
  2. Explore events - find upcoming hackathons that match your interests and skill level.
  3. Join or form a team - either join an existing team using a code, create your own team, or start solo.
  4. Collaborate - use the task board, chat, and project page to work with your teammates.
  5. Submit your project - showcase your work in the project gallery and get feedback from the community.

Our Team

HackHub was created by a group of passionate students from MMU who experienced the challenges of joining hackathons firsthand. We believe that everyone deserves a fair chance to learn, build, and shine - and we built HackHub to make that happen.

If you'd like to contribute or have suggestions, feel free to send us feedback or reach out via our social channels.
